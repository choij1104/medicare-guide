## Full description (≤4000)

```
Medicare Guide explains Medicare in plain Korean and plain English, so Korean-American seniors and their families can understand their choices without depending on a sales agent. It is written for Texas, with Texas phone numbers and programs.

IMPORTANT: This is an independent educational app made by HAKOYA LLC. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Medicare, the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), the Social Security Administration (SSA) or any government agency, and it does not sell insurance. Figures are summarized from official sources: medicare.gov, cms.gov and ssa.gov. For official information, visit those sites or call 1-800-MEDICARE.

WHAT IS INSIDE
• Basics: Parts A, B, C and D, and the four cost words you must know (premium, deductible, copay/coinsurance, out-of-pocket maximum).
• Two buckets: a simple picture of how a deductible differs from an out-of-pocket maximum, with a worked Part B example.
• Key amounts for 2026 and 2027, each marked "Official" or "Projected". Projected 2027 amounts are estimates until CMS announces them.
• Advantage vs Supplement: Original Medicare + Medigap (Plan G, Plan N, High-Deductible G) + Part D compared with Medicare Advantage, and the 6-month Medigap open enrollment warning.
• Find my fit: answer 8 short questions to see which direction may suit you, then enter the quotes you received to compare yearly costs for a light year and a bad year.
• Help paying: Extra Help, Medicare Savings Programs and Medicaid, with a quick Extra Help self-check.
• Scam check: a checklist of common Medicare scam signs and where to report them.
• Dates and phones: enrollment periods, how to enroll by phone, and free official numbers (Medicare, Texas HICAP, Social Security, Senior Medicare Patrol) with one-tap copy.

BUILT FOR SENIORS
• Large text and a calm, simple layout.
• Switch between Korean and English at any time.
• Works fully offline. No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Nothing you type leaves your phone.

This app is for education only. It is not insurance sales, and not legal, medical or financial advice. Before you choose a plan, talk to Texas HICAP for free, neutral counseling at 1-800-252-9240.
```
