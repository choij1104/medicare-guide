Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026 · HAKOYA LLC, Selma, Texas, USA · admin@hakoyallc.com

English

The Medicare Guide app does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. The app works entirely offline and does not send any data to HAKOYA LLC or to anyone else. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Numbers you enter in the calculators stay on your device and are not saved. The app stores only your language choice (Korean or English) on your device; uninstalling the app deletes it. Links to medicare.gov, cms.gov and ssa.gov open in your web browser, where those sites' own privacy policies apply.

Medicare Guide is an independent educational app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Medicare, CMS, the Social Security Administration, or any government agency. It is not intended for children.
